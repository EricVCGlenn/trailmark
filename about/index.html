About Eric

Meet Eric Glenn

Founder of Trailmark Insurance Advisors and your local, independent insurance advisor in Denton, Texas.

Hi, I'm Eric Glenn.

I'm the founder of Trailmark Insurance Advisors and a licensed Texas property and casualty agent. Since 2020, I've helped families and business owners across North Texas save money on their home, auto, condo, renters, landlord, motorcycle and business insurance.

My biggest win so far? Saving one client $5,726 a year on their home and auto insurance.*

From the Stage to Insurance

My path to insurance wasn't exactly traditional. Before insurance, I spent my career in dance, as a professional ballet dancer and the artistic director of a dance studio.

When my family and I moved from Houston to the Flower Mound area in 2020, I took a job managing a local insurance agency. I quickly found that what I loved about teaching dance carried right over: taking something complicated, breaking it into clear steps and helping people feel confident about it. Insurance just happens to involve fewer pirouettes.

Why I Started Trailmark

The agency I managed was a captive agency, which meant I could offer my clients only one insurance company. When that company wasn't the right fit for someone, my hands were tied, and I hated sending people away without a better option.

I started Trailmark so that never happens again. As an independent agency, I can shop your coverage with Progressive, Travelers, Liberty Mutual, The Hartford and National General, so you get the right coverage at the best price available, not just the best price one company offers.

Trailmark is built on two things: personal service and a commitment to excellence. My goal is to help my neighbors across Denton County and North Texas find coverage that's truly personalized to them.

What Working With Me Looks Like

I'm at my best when someone needs solid advice from a person they can trust. Whether you're buying your first home, adding a teen driver, opening a business or just tired of your renewal going up every year, I'll explain the what, the why and the how of your coverage in plain English. You'll know exactly what you're paying for, why it matters and what your options are.

  • Quotes from multiple top-rated carriers, compared side by side
  • Honest recommendations, even when that means a smaller policy
  • Free policy reviews, at renewal or anytime your life changes
  • A real person to call or email, Monday through Friday

Outside the Office

I'm married with two young daughters, and my favorite place to be is wherever they are.

I still perform and teach ballet at a local studio. And if you find yourself in Elizabethtown, Kentucky during the holiday season, you might catch me on stage in The Nutcracker, a tradition I've been part of for 17 years.

I'm also a proud University of Oklahoma graduate, so on fall Saturdays you'll almost always find me watching OU football.
