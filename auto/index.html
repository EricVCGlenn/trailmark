Auto Insurance FAQ

Straight answers to the auto-insurance questions North Texas drivers ask most.

Is car insurance required in Texas?
Yes. Texas law requires drivers to carry at least 30/60/25 liability coverage — $30,000 per injured person, $60,000 per accident, and $25,000 for property damage. Most drivers carry more, because those minimums rarely cover a serious accident. We'll help you decide how much protection actually makes sense for you.
How can an independent agent save me money on auto insurance?
Because we're independent, we quote your policy with several carriers at once — Liberty Mutual, Travelers, Progressive, GEICO, and more — and show you the best fit. Instead of taking one company's rate, you get a comparison, plus every discount you qualify for (multi-policy, safe driver, multi-car, and more).
Should I bundle my auto and home insurance?
Usually, yes. Bundling auto and home (or renters/condo) with the same carrier typically earns a meaningful multi-policy discount and makes your coverage easier to manage. We'll run it both bundled and separate so you can see the real difference.
What's the difference between liability, collision, and comprehensive?
Liability pays for damage and injuries you cause to others. Collision pays to repair your own car after an accident. Comprehensive covers non-collision events like hail, theft, fire, or hitting a deer — which matters a lot in North Texas hail country. We'll walk through which you need.
Do I need full coverage if my car is paid off?
Not necessarily. If you own the car outright, dropping collision and comprehensive is your choice — but if you couldn't comfortably replace the vehicle out of pocket, keeping them is often worth it. If you have a loan or lease, the lender will require full coverage.
How do accidents, tickets, or a teen driver affect my rate?
All three can raise premiums, but by how much varies a lot between carriers — which is exactly where shopping independently pays off. Adding a teen driver especially: some companies are far friendlier to young drivers than others, and we know which ones to check.
