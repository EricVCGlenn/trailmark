Privacy Policy

Trailmark Insurance Advisors LLC

We collect information you provide when requesting insurance information, obtaining a quote, contacting our agency, or receiving policy assistance. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, and information needed to quote or service insurance.

We use this information to respond to requests, obtain insurance quotes, assist with applications, service policies, and communicate about appointments, coverage, and renewals.

We may share information as necessary with insurance carriers and service providers to obtain quotes, process applications, and service insurance, or as required by law.

Text Messaging Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties, except service providers necessary to deliver our text messaging services.

We use text messaging for customer service communications, including requested quotes, appointment reminders, policy updates, and renewal reminders. We do not send promotional offers by text.

Information Protection

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to or correction of information you have provided, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

To stop text messages, reply STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or email eric@texastrailmark.com.
