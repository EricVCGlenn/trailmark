Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Trailmark Insurance Advisors ("Trailmark," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through texastrailmark.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill out a quote request or contact form, call or email us, we collect details such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, the coverage you're interested in and anything you include in your message.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services we use may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and prepare insurance quotes
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your request, using the method you prefer
  • To share necessary information with insurance companies when you ask us to obtain quotes or coverage for you
  • To operate, secure and improve our website
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with insurance carriers and service providers as needed to provide the quotes and services you request, or when required by law.

Service providers we use

  • Formspree processes the forms on our website and delivers your submission to us by email.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and deliver our website.
  • Google Fonts and Google Maps provide fonts and the map on our contact page, and may collect technical information under Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising cookies. Third-party services such as Google Maps may set their own cookies when you interact with them. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information such as Social Security or bank account numbers through our website forms.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the contact information you've submitted, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing eric@texastrailmark.com or calling (214) 531-7932. If you receive a text message from us, you can reply STOP to opt out.

Children's privacy

Our website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
