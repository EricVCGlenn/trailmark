Motorcycle Insurance From a Local, Independent Advisor

North Texas is great riding country, from the farm roads around Lake Ray Roberts to long highway trips across the state. Trailmark Insurance Advisors compares motorcycle insurance from multiple carriers for cruisers, sport bikes, touring bikes, dual-sport bikes, trikes and scooters.

Motorcycle coverage works differently from car insurance, so we'll help you choose the right protection for your bike, your riding style and the custom parts you've added.

What Motorcycle Insurance Covers

Liability

Pays for injuries and damage you cause to others. Texas requires the same 30/60/25 minimum liability for motorcycles as for cars.

Collision

Repairs or replaces your bike after an accident.

Comprehensive

Covers theft, hail, fire, vandalism and animal collisions.

Uninsured and underinsured motorist

Protects you when a driver without enough insurance causes an accident.

Medical payments or PIP

Helps pay medical bills for you and your passenger.

Custom parts and equipment

Protects aftermarket chrome, exhaust, seats, saddlebags and custom paint.

Riding gear

Covers helmets, jackets and other protective gear.

Roadside assistance and trip interruption

Help with towing and lodging if you break down far from home.

Riding in Texas

Texas riders face heavy traffic, high summer heat and drivers who don't always see motorcycles. Uninsured motorist coverage and strong medical protection are especially important, because injuries in a motorcycle accident can be serious and the other driver may not carry enough insurance.

Ways to Save on Motorcycle Insurance

  • Completing a motorcycle safety course

  • Riding experience and a claims-free history
  • Paid-in-full discounts

Motorcycle Insurance FAQs

Is motorcycle insurance required in Texas?

Yes. Texas requires motorcycle owners to carry liability insurance with at least 30/60/25 limits, the same minimums as passenger cars.

Does my auto policy cover my motorcycle?

No. Motorcycles need their own policy. Many carriers offer a discount when you bundle motorcycle insurance with your auto or home policy.

Are my custom parts covered?

Standard policies include only a small amount of custom parts and equipment coverage. If you've added chrome, exhaust, seats, saddlebags or custom paint, we can help you add enough coverage to protect your investment.
