Motorhomes vs. Towables

Motorized RVs need their own liability coverage, just like a car. For travel trailers and fifth wheels, liability usually extends from the vehicle towing it, but physical damage to the trailer itself typically requires its own coverage.

RV Insurance FAQs

Do I need separate insurance for a travel trailer?

Your auto policy may extend liability to a trailer you're towing, but it usually won't cover damage to the trailer itself or your belongings inside. A travel trailer policy fills those gaps.

What is full-timer RV insurance?

If you live in your RV for most of the year, full-timer coverage adds protection similar to a homeowners policy, including personal liability and broader personal property coverage.

Is my RV covered when it's in storage?

It can be. Many policies let you reduce coverage while your RV is stored to lower your premium, while keeping comprehensive protection against theft, hail and fire.
