Text Messaging Terms & Conditions

Trailmark Insurance Advisors LLC

Program Name

Trailmark Insurance Advisors Customer Service Text Messaging.

Program Description

By opting in, you agree to receive customer service text messages from Trailmark Insurance Advisors LLC related to insurance quote requests, appointments, applications, policy servicing, and renewals. We do not send promotional offers through this program.

Message Frequency and Charges

Messages may be recurring. Message frequency varies based on your requests and insurance servicing needs.

Message and data rates may apply.

Consent

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing insurance. You may contact our agency by email for assistance without enrolling in text messaging.

Opting Out and Getting Help

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You may receive a final message confirming your opt-out, after which no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Reply HELP for assistance, or email eric@texastrailmark.com.

Carrier Disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Please review our Privacy Policy for information about how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Insurance Requests

Text messages do not bind, change, or cancel insurance coverage. Coverage requests require confirmation from an authorized agency representative or insurance carrier.
